# Estimator guide for the business owner

Use this guide to manage the questions and indicative prices visitors see. Every estimate remains subject to a site visit. A saved price change applies to new estimates; quotes already sent keep their original figures.

## How a price is built

**Base price → answers can change it → location can adjust it → range and rounding.**

Example: 1,000 sq ft × ₹2,000 = ₹20,00,000. A selected finish may add ₹150 per sq ft. A 5% location increase is applied after those choices, then the displayed range is calculated.

## Add a service

1. Open **Estimator** and choose **Create your first service from a template** (or **Create from template**).
2. Pick the closest starting point and give the service a clear visitor-facing name.
3. Choose whether visitors see a price estimate or request a site inspection first.
4. Review each numbered section and use **Try it** before saving.

## Add questions and choices

Keep the main flow short. Add optional details under **Optional extras**. Write questions as a visitor would read them and give every answer a clear label. Use the up/down controls to set the order. A question can use a simple condition to appear only after a related answer.

## Change prices safely

Read the summary at the top, check the impact preview, then try several answers in the live preview. The impact table shows the previous and proposed figures for sample projects. Save only after the examples match your intended price. A placeholder-rate warning means the client still needs to supply approved prices.

## Add a location surcharge

Add the area names visitors may enter, including common spelling variants. Enter a positive percentage to increase the total or a negative percentage to reduce it. The default row always applies 0% to other areas.

## Test before saving

Choose answers in **Try it** and read the plain-language calculation steps. Check a small, typical and large project, optional choices, and each location. Review the ready-to-publish checklist and resolve red items before turning a service on.

## What “site inspection first” means

Visitors will not see an online price. They can send their details so the team can review the work and arrange an inspection. No price fields are needed for this service.

## If a price looks wrong

Check the service's base price and unit first, then the chosen answer's price change, location percentage, minimum charge, range and rounding. Compare the explanation step by step. If the numbers still do not match your approved price list, leave the service inactive and ask the project administrator to review the configuration.
