## 3. About text (base copy)
M/s. Vakratund Construction is a civil engineering concern specialized in project management, repairs and rehabilitation of RCC structures, and construction of new buildings. The firm was established in 2003 and has carried out turnkey construction projects as well as repair and rehabilitation projects in the region. Its aim is to provide genuine civil engineering services and solutions to corporate offices and co-operative housing societies facing problems such as terrace leakages, plaster damage and structural failure of RCC members.

The firm has an excellent record in redevelopment of buildings and societies along with repairs and rehabilitation, working in tandem with consultants and clients and executing jobs of varying magnitude within the stipulated time frame. Its civil engineers and supervisors are well versed in structural strengthening, plastering, terrace waterproofing, interior work, exterior painting and plumbing. The firm has also executed several government projects, maintenance works and road works.
